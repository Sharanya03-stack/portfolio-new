The Meme Equation: Simulating Virality in the Age of Algorithms

Authors: Sharanya N, Arya Sham Pandit, Khan saif S • Department of Science and Humanities, Sri Sairam College of Engineering, Bangalore

Investigates information diffusion and short-lifecycle viral content across social media platforms. Adapts the epidemiological SIR (Susceptible–Infected–Recovered) differential equation model to treat network users as interconnected nodes, evaluating sharing velocity (β) against attention decay (γ) across a 10,000-node simulated community to understand algorithmic reach, influencer amplification, and content decay.
